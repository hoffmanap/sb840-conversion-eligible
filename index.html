SB 840 Conversion Candidates

Which El Paso office, retail, and warehouse buildings could become housing

Texas SB 840 (89th Legislature) forces cities over 150,000 people to allow qualifying commercial buildings to convert to multifamily or mixed-use residential — no rezoning case, no added parking, no traffic study. This is every El Paso parcel the county's own appraisal records say might qualify, built from EPCAD parcel, improvement, and property data.

What the bill requires

Not a blanket rezoning

SB 840 (Local Government Code Ch. 218) only opens this door for buildings that clear a specific set of conditions.

  • 01

    Office, retail, or warehouse use

    The building's current, on-record use has to be one of these three categories — restaurants, auto shops, hotels and similar commercial uses don't qualify.

  • 02

    Built 5+ years ago

    The structure must have been constructed at least five years before the conversion date.

  • 03

    65% of the building, minimum, residential

    At least 65% of total building area, and 65% of each occupiable floor, must convert to residential use.

  • 04

    Nonresidential capped near 35%

    In a mixed-use conversion, nonresidential space can make up at most the remaining share of the building.

  • 05

    Away from airports and bases

    Parcels within 3,000 ft of an airport or military base, or 15,000 ft of a designated military overlay zone, are excluded.

What owners get in return

The city loses most of its usual discretion

In exchange for meeting those conditions, these projects are approved administratively, not case-by-case.

Density and design

  • Up to 145 units per acre — El Paso's applicable ceiling under the bill.
  • No more than one parking space per unit; no parking structure required.
  • Setbacks capped at whatever a commercial building on the same site would face.

Process and fees

  • Administrative approval — no council vote, no rezoning case.
  • No traffic impact analysis and no traffic-mitigation payment required.
  • No new impact fee unless the land was already subject to one.
